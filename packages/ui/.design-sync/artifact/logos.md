# Logos

The Visvine logo: three ringed nodes joined into a V. Paint it only in the brand green (`vv-color-brand`, #78d870), never in the chosen accent, and never redraw or restyle it. In a product surface, use the `Logo` component; these files are for everything else.

- `visvine-mark.svg`: the green glyph on transparent, for light grounds. Vector, drawn by the `Logo` component (`variant="mark"`).
- `visvine-tile.svg`: the white glyph on the green rounded square, the app icon. Vector, drawn by the `Logo` component (`variant="tile"`).
- `brand-icon.png`: the tile at 1932px, the original raster the web app and desktop icons are made from.
- `ios-logo.png`: the green glyph at 256px, the original raster on transparent.
- `ios-app-icon.png`: the tile at 1024px with square corners; iOS rounds them itself.
